Food Confirmation & Log

The one screen every way of adding food ends on (IA 3): search, barcode and photo from Add Food, a recipe from Recipe Detail, and an entry already on Today. Same skeleton, same portion control, same place for the action; only named slots change. Built only from Design System v1.

  • Stage 5 · Screens, 03 of 05 · revision 2, after one critique
  • Phone frame: 390 × 844 with iPhone safe areas; the portion controls work
  • Sample figures are illustrative, never real nutrition data

At a glance

Revision 2. Revision 1 was generated in one pass from Design System v1 and scored 25/40 in one /impeccable critique run: 0 P0, 1 P1 and 4 P2 issues, all fixed (details below). The trace below shows how each of the three kinds of arrival (a new item, a recipe, an existing entry) fills the same skeleton without contradicting the others.

What the screen does

  • Shows what an item is before it counts: where the number came from, the kcal for your portion, and the macros behind it.
  • Lets you set the portion in the unit you think in; every figure follows it.
  • Takes three kinds of arrival (a new item, a recipe, an entry already logged) and changes only what differs between them.
  • Always ends on Today: “Log it” adds, “Save changes” updates, “Remove entry” deletes.

Decided here

  • One skeleton with named slots; a path can fill a slot, never add a layout of its own (next section).
  • “Back” in the top start corner: a step inside a task goes back, while a task (Add Food) closes.
  • The recipe thumbnail joins the system as M1, the labelled image placeholder.
  • Two actions stack with the primary last, so “Log it” never moves. On an edit, “Cancel” and “Remove entry” share the top row, far from “Save changes”.

Still open

  • Undo after “Log it” or “Remove entry”, and “discard changes?” on Back: v2 states (IA Flow 4).
  • Closed at IA 5: the serving count means the servings you are eating, and it carries over, so this screen opens at whatever Recipe Detail was left on — 1 in frame D.
  • Corrected while building IA 5: frame D’s macros were 18 / 52 / 14 g, while the card the same dish is chosen from on 04 Recipes & Filter reads 26 / 48 / 12 g. One dish cannot carry two sets of figures through a handoff whose whole claim is continuity, so the card’s figures are canonical and this frame now matches them. The 05 build asserts the three screens agree before it writes a byte.
  • Real images: M1 placeholders until the Krea pass.

Heuristics, applied while building

Nielsen’s ten heuristics served as constraints during generation; the single /impeccable critique run tested them afterwards.

HeuristicApplied as
1 · Visibility of system statusThe source is named on every path. Figures change as the portion changes, and the change is announced. The context line marks an edit, and the action label says what will happen to Today.
2 · Match with the real world“Your portion”, “1 slice”, “1 bar”, “Logged at 08:10 as 40 g”. Units follow how the item is measured.
3 · User control and freedomBack on every path commits nothing, and on an edit it says “Cancel”. “Remove entry” is at the top, away from “Save changes”. A wrongly identified photo can be retaken or searched for instead.
4 · Consistency and standardsOne skeleton for five arrivals. “Log this” ends on the same “Log it”. Source first, as on Today. The recipe path uses Recipe Detail’s stepper.
5 · Error preventionName and source before any figure. A portion check before anything is logged. The destructive action is out of the thumb zone.
6 · Recognition over recallThe logged amount stays in view while you edit. Units are named, and shares sit beside the grams.
7 · Flexibility and efficiencyType the amount or step it. When the suggested portion is right, it’s one tap to log.
8 · Aesthetic and minimalist designOne serif name, one block, one control, one primary action. No micronutrients, badges or scores.
9 · Recognize and recover from errorsSearch instead (or retake) for a misidentified photo, Back for a wrong result, Save or Remove for a past mistake. An invalid amount falls back to the last valid one. Undo is v2.
10 · Help and documentationOne helper line, only where the risk is: a photo estimate.

Detector findings

24 findings, none fixed, and 20 of them are identical on 02 Add Food. They are false positives: padding and font shorthands set through var() tokens the detector can’t resolve (the action bar, the unit select, whose padding sits on the select by design), plus the device frame and the notes. The rest are deliberate: the Paper brand color, the striped M1 placeholder and the J1 hatch in the shared CSS. One flag is about the rhythm of these notes’ prose, not product copy. The in-browser overlay couldn’t run: the preview serves local files as data: snapshots, and the live server doesn’t serve project files.

Images: one slot, on one path

Image placeholders go only where the IA or the system calls for a picture. On this screen that is the recipe thumbnail, and only when arriving from Recipe Detail.

  • M1, added to the system before use: 135° stripes in Oat Deep and Paper with a Paper label reading “IMAGE · UNVALIDATED (§7)”, rebuilt from tokens after the pattern in 03-direction-preview.html.
  • Same crop as the recipe card (ratio-recipe-image, 4:3, still provisional), so the thumbnail is recognisably the card you chose.
  • No slot on the other paths: not for search, barcode or photo results, and not when a recipe entry is reopened from Today.
  • Final pass: real placeholder images come from Krea after all five screens are laid out. Until then, nothing is generated, downloaded or sourced.
