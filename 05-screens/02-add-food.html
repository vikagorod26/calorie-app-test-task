Add Food

Story 1’s way in (IA 2), one tap from Today. Three ways to add food: search, barcode or photo. The switch stays put while the panel below it changes, and every way ends on the same Food Confirmation screen. Built only from Design System v1.

  • Stage 5 · Screens, 02 of 05 · revision 2, after one critique
  • Phone frame: 390 × 844 with iPhone safe areas; scroll inside the frame
  • Sample figures are illustrative, never real nutrition data

At a glance

Revision 2. Revision 1 was generated in one pass from Design System v1 and scored 24/40 in one /impeccable critique run; its two P1 and two P2 issues are fixed (details below). The screen also settles two questions the system audit left open: A7, how the mode switch is announced, and A8, how it fits at 320 px.

What the screen does

  • Offers three ways to add food (search, barcode, photo) behind one switch that stays in place.
  • Names the source once above the results and gives each one its serving and kcal, so near-duplicates can be told apart.
  • Never dead-ends: a search with no match points to a photo estimate, and a found barcode says what it found.
  • Sends every way to the same Food Confirmation screen. Nothing is logged from this screen.

Decided here

  • A7: the mode switch is a set of tabs (tablist), because each mode swaps the whole panel.
  • A8: text labels at 320 px and up, icons only below that. Icons plus labels didn’t fit a 360 px phone.
  • Camera UI joins the system: a viewfinder (V1) with a found state, a bottom action bar (S3) and a no-result message (E1).

Still open

  • Camera permission, “no barcode found” and search on first open: v2 states.
  • Typing a barcode number: the button is here, its input state isn’t built yet.
  • “Retake photo” moves to Food Confirmation, where the estimate is reviewed.

A7 and A8, resolved in context

The Design System v1 audit left both open until a real screen could decide them. Both are now system changes, recorded in components-v1.html F3, tokens-v1.md and audit-v1.md §6.

A7 · Tabs, not toggle buttons

  • What the switch does: each mode replaces the whole panel below it: a results list or a camera. On Recipes, the two filter modes swap their filter controls. Both are tabs.
  • Markup: role="tablist" named “How to add food”; each segment a tab with aria-selected and a roving tabindex; the panel a tabpanel labelled by its tab.
  • Keys: arrows move focus, Enter or Space selects. Selection is manual because Barcode and Photo start the camera, which shouldn’t happen just by arrowing past.
  • Rejected: aria-pressed (announces three separate toggles) and radiogroup (implies a form value, not a panel change).

A8 · Labels at 320 and up, icons below

  • Measured in this screen, with Inter loaded: icon and label need about 104 px per segment, which doesn’t fit a 360 px phone (101 px available). Labels alone fit at 320 px, with 7.8 px to spare in the tightest case, “Barcode” selected at 600.
  • Under bp-screen-narrow (320) the segments go icon-only, the word stays as the accessible name, and the gap closes: 49 px wide at 195 px, which is a 390 px phone at 200% zoom.
  • No new breakpoint and no new raw value: the rule reuses the one S2 already uses.
  • Found on the way: the S1 body column grew to fit the switch, so a 320 px screen would have scrolled sideways. The column is now minmax(0,1fr), the same fix as audit P2.

Heuristics, applied while building

Nielsen’s ten heuristics served as constraints during generation; the single /impeccable critique run tested them afterwards.

HeuristicApplied as
1 · Visibility of system statusThe current mode is marked by fill, outline, weight and aria-selected. The result count sits above the list and is announced when it changes. Barcode says it scans on its own, then shows “Found: …” before it moves on.
2 · Match with the real worldPlain words: “per 170 g”, “Take photo”, “the whole plate”, “Type the barcode number”. Servings in the units on the package.
3 · User control and freedomClose is always on screen, and the system back gesture does the same. Nothing is logged here, so choosing the wrong result costs one step back from Food Confirmation.
4 · Consistency and standardsA result is the same row as a Today entry. “Take photo” is the same button as “Log it”. The switch uses the same “chosen” look as tabs and chips.
5 · Error preventionServing and kcal on every result, so near-duplicates don’t get mixed up. A wide frame for barcodes, a square for plates. The photo step promises a portion check.
6 · Recognition over recallModes are named, not icons, wherever the words fit. The placeholder shows an example query. Source and serving are printed on each result.
7 · Flexibility and efficiencyAny method is one tap from the others. Barcode needs no capture tap. Recent foods stay out of scope (04-conclusions.md §2).
8 · Aesthetic and minimalist designOne serif title, one switch, one panel. No promo, no lock icons, no confidence badges.
9 · Recognize and recover from errorsA search with no match says so and offers a photo estimate. Typing the barcode number is a recovery route for a failed scan; its state, and “no barcode found”, come with v2 error states.
10 · Help and documentationEach camera mode has one sentence of instruction next to the camera, not a tutorial.

Images: none on this screen, on purpose

Image placeholders go only where the IA or the system calls for a picture. Add Food has no image slot.

  • The dark camera area is not an image placeholder. It stands in for live video, which no generated image should imitate. Hybrid §7 allows logging screens “only the camera viewfinder”.
  • Search results have no thumbnails. C2 carries none, and results must look like the Today entries they become.
  • Image slots, elsewhere: the recipe thumbnail on Food Confirmation (Flow 3 only), recipe card images and the Recipe Detail hero — all filled with real dish photographs at the Stage 7 Krea pass. This screen still has none: its camera area is live video, not an image.
