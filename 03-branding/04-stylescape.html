The Measured Kitchen

Words are warm. Figures are exact. Brand stylescape for a calorie calculator & recipe app

Idea

A warm kitchen that keeps exact books.

The app has two jobs: count what you eat, and help you find what to cook. Logging needs the precision of a ledger. Choosing a recipe needs the warmth of a cookbook, and numbers that match your goals. The Measured Kitchen speaks one language, warm in tone and exact in numbers whether you're logging or planning, so a recipe you find and a meal you log always feel like the same place.

Colors

Kitchen neutrals, one green to act.

A warm oat ground ties every screen together. Numbers sit on clean paper in deep ink. Pine green is saved for the things you tap, and three pantry tones mark protein, carbs and fat.

References

Where the idea comes from.

Real screens from shipped nutrition apps, collected on Mobbin. The Measured Kitchen takes the ledger's discipline from one family and the cookbook's warmth from the other.

The LedgerMyFitnessPal

We took: numbers in aligned columns, a single action color, and a recipe page that ends in a "Log to diary" button.

The CookbookLifesum & Noom

We took: warm oat grounds, a bookish serif voice, calm pacing, and diet-tag chips for the recipe filter.

Explored and set aside

Yazio, Cal AI and MacroFactor showed how bold numbers can be. We set them aside because streaks and mascots sit outside the two user stories this app is built around, and custom illustration works against the brief's goal of minimal manual work.
