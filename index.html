The Measured Kitchen

A calorie tracker that also finds recipes that suit you, then logs them just as precisely as you planned them.

  • Story 1As a user, I want to calculate the amount of calories in a dish or a specific product.
  • Story 2As a user, I want to find a recipe for a dish that is suitable for me.
