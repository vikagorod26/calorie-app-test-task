# Project: Calorie Calculator App — UX/UI Trainee Test Task

## What this is
Test task for a UX/UI Trainee Designer position. Goal: demonstrate an 
AI-native design workflow using Claude Code, from research to final screens.

## User stories to cover
1. As a user, I want to calculate the amount of calories in a dish or a specific product.
2. As a user, I want to find a recipe for a dish that is suitable for me.

## Constraints from the brief
- All deliverables must be in English.
- Process constraint: prefer AI-generated/AI-assisted output over manual hand-crafting (e.g. no manual Figma work) — this applies to HOW work gets done, not to the rigor or quality bar, which stays high throughout (research grounding, accessibility checks, traceability).

## Platform decision
Cross-platform, brand-first design system — not strictly following iOS HIG 
or Material Design 3. Rationale: the brief emphasizes branding and systemic 
thinking over platform-native engineering; building two native systems 
would double manual effort, contradicting "minimize manual work".

## Notes for Claude Code
- Always check /01-research/ before making branding or UX decisions.
- Keep all screen generation within a single session/context to avoid visual drift.
- **Standing rule — traceability:** applies to every stage from here on, not just IA, but not uniformly to every kind of decision — split it in two:
  - **Structural/functional decisions** (screen list, flows, IA, what data is shown, what features exist) — before deciding, cross-check against both (1) this brief and (2) the findings in `/01-research/`. Every such decision must be traceable to one or both. If it isn't — a scope trim, a gap the research doesn't cover — flag it explicitly as a judgment call rather than presenting it as evidenced.
  - **Pure aesthetic choices during branding** (specific color shades, font-pairing "feel", visual mood, decorative detail) — these do not need a literal citation per micro-choice; forcing one produces fake rigor. Instead, ground the overall direction in the audience/tone that research established (who these users are, what tone earns their trust vs. what reads as the paywall-happy, cluttered incumbents they're frustrated with) and call out where a specific choice follows from that tone. Judgment calls are expected and fine here — just distinguish "grounded in research tone/audience" from "arbitrary," don't cite a source for every shade of green.

### Stage 1 — Research
Full synthesis lives in `01-research/04-conclusions.md` — read it before IA, branding, or design-system work. Key hypotheses: logging speed/low-friction beats feature depth; trust hinges on the accuracy of whichever logging method is used, and paywalling that method is a top betrayal signal; recipe discovery is a genuinely unserved need across competitors; and the handoff from "find a recipe" to "log what I ate" is the sharpest available differentiator. Prioritized features: P0 core loop = manual search, barcode scan, AI-photo logging, macro breakdown, personalized calorie/macro targets; P1 differentiators = recipe discovery filtered by both diet tags and macro-target matching, plus one-action recipe-to-log integration. Nice-to-haves (voice logging, quick-add shortcuts, diet-mode toggles, generic recipe browsing) are explicitly out of scope for this project.

Files:

Reddit was not reachable. Off-story complaints (ads, billing, redesign churn) are kept in an "Out of scope" table.
- `01-research/02-audience-5w.md` — three 5W segments with job stories: Daily Calorie Tracker (primary → story 1), Suitability-Seeking Home Cook (secondary → story 2), Macro-Conscious Meal Planner (bridge between the stories). ### Stage 2 — Information Architecture
Full document: `02-information-architecture/information-architecture.md` (includes a traceability self-audit against all research files and `PRODUCT.md`).

- **Scope:** exactly five screens covering the two user stories. Onboarding and Profile/Settings were cut (they serve neither story). Where saved targets and diet preferences come from is out of scope — a flagged judgment call; a profile screen would be a deliberate 6th screen later.
- **Screens:**
  1. **Today / Daily Log** (home) — remaining/consumed calories and macros vs. today's target, chronological list of today's entries, persistent one-tap entry to Add Food. Shared entry point both stories return to.
  2. **Add Food** — one entry point with three modes: Search, Barcode Scan, Photo Scan (AI result shown for review before accepting).
  3. **Food Confirmation & Log** — food/dish name, calories, full macro breakdown, editable portion/serving, "Log it". The single convergence screen for every logging method, including recipes.
  4. **Recipes & Filter** — two filter modes on one screen ("By diet & goals" tags and "By macro match" numeric targets), pre-filled from saved targets/preferences; results show name, image, calories/serving and matched criteria.
  5. **Recipe Detail** — ingredients, steps, image, per-serving nutrition adjustable by serving count, and an always-present "Log this".
- **User flows:**
  - **Flow 1 — Story 1 (calculate calories):** Today → Add Food → Search / Barcode / Photo → Food Confirmation & Log → "Log it" → Today.
  - **Flow 2 — Story 2 (find a suitable recipe):** Today → Recipes & Filter → choose a filter mode, results update in place → Recipe Detail.
- **Intersection point (Flow 3):** Recipe Detail → "Log this" → Food Confirmation & Log (pre-filled with the recipe's calculated nutrition) → "Log it" → Today, where the recipe appears like any manually logged item. It reuses Flow 1's final screen and action, so the handoff is one flow, not a parallel logging path.
- **Structural rules:** (1) logging is reachable in one tap from anywhere; (2) a recipe detail view never dead-ends — it always resolves into Food Confirmation & Log; (3) *(added at Stage 5, after the Today critique)* a logged entry is never final.
- **Flow 4 — Correct a logged entry (added at Stage 5):** Today → tap an entry → Food Confirmation & Log (pre-filled; "Save changes" / "Remove entry") → Today. Evidence: barcode/photo/database accuracy findings and H2. Reusing Screen 3 is a flagged judgment call.
- **Flagged judgment calls:** the mode-switcher pattern on Add Food, one shared confirmation screen for all methods, chronological ordering on Today, and the review step for photo results (the accuracy risk is evidenced; the review-step response is not).

### Stage 3 — Branding

#### Working log
- Three visual directions grounded in real Mobbin screens (local copies in `03-branding/refs/`), documented in `03-branding/01-direction-options.md` + `.html`: A · Clinical Ledger (MyFitnessPal — transparent data, strongest for trust/H2 but reads as the incumbent), B · Oat & Serif (Lifesum/Noom — warm editorial, strongest for recipes/H3 but weak for fast logging and numeric trust), C · Bold Coach (Yazio/Cal AI/MacroFactor — high-contrast, big numerals, strongest for glanceable logging/H1 but gamified tone risks trust). #### Complete — binding reference
- **Direction: The Measured Kitchen.** A hybrid of A · Clinical Ledger's numerical trust (aligned, checkable figures; one action color) and B · Oat & Serif's cookbook warmth (oat ground, editorial serif, calm pacing). C · Bold Coach's gamification and custom illustration are excluded (outside the two user stories; conflict with "minimize manual work").
- **Unifying rule: "Words are warm, figures are exact."** Every nutrition figure gets one identical treatment (Inter, tabular, ink, never serif) on logging and recipe screens alike; warmth lives only in words, ground and imagery; contexts shift emphasis, never tokens.
- **Core palette:** ground Oat `#F2EDE6` · paper `#FFFDF9` · ink `#1F2421` · Pine `#2F5245` (the only action color; Pine Light `#DCE6DF` for a chosen diet tag or macro-match filter) · macro marks Terracotta `#B0513A` (protein), Ochre `#A87A1F` (carbs), Slate `#4D6A85` (fat) — marks only, labels stay in ink.
- **Typography:** Newsreader for voice (recipe names, headlines); Inter for interface and all figures. Numbers are never set in the serif.
- **Copy guardrails from the research consistency check:** figures are presented as consistent and checkable, not promised as accurate (AI-photo logging is estimate-based); "core logging never feels locked away" (monetization itself stays undecided); the "no words over the plate" imagery rule applies to recipe imagery, not photo-scan UI; over-target/status tone is still open.
- **Files:** `03-branding/04-stylescape.html` is the final branding presentation; `03-branding/02-hybrid-direction.md` holds the full rationale and traceability.
- **Binding reference:** this direction is now binding for the Design System and Screens stages. Any deviation (palette, type roles, the unifying rule) must be justified against research or the brief, and reflected back into `04-stylescape.html` (and `02-hybrid-direction.md`) so the presentation never drifts from what ships.
