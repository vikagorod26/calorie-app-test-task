Chickpea & spinach stew

Makes 4 servings

Ingredients

For all 4 servings, as the recipe is written.

  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • 1onion, finely chopped
  • 3garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tspground cumin
  • 1 tspsmoked paprika
  • 400 gchopped tomatoes
  • 200 gred lentils, rinsed
  • 400 gchickpeas, drained
  • 200 gspinach
  • 2 tbspnutritional yeast
  • —Sea salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Warm the oil in a wide pan over a medium heat and soften the onion for 8 minutes.
  2. Stir in the garlic, cumin and paprika and cook for a minute, until it smells toasted.
  3. Add the tomatoes, lentils and 600 ml of water. Bring to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes, until the lentils collapse.
  4. Tip in the chickpeas and warm through for 5 minutes.
  5. Stir the spinach through in handfuls until it wilts, then season and add the nutritional yeast.
